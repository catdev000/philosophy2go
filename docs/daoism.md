::: details Daoism TLDR (Short summary)
Dao — the natural, ineffable "Way" or underlying flow of the universe. Rather than forcing outcomes through rigid rules or ambition, it teaches effortless action, humility, simplicity, and alignment with nature's rhythms.
:::

::: article 
## The Dao (道) — The Way.
![Daoism Yin Yang Symbol](/images/daoism_yin_yang.png)
The nameless, formless source and pattern of everything. It can't be fully defined or grasped intellectually; you align with it rather than comprehend it. "The Dao that can be spoken is not the eternal Dao."
:::

::: article 
## Wu Wei (無為) — Effortless action
![River and Forest Image](/images/daoism_river.png)
Acting without force, striving, or ego-driven interference. Water is the classic metaphor — soft, yielding, yet it wears away stone.
:::

::: article 
## Ziran (自然) — Naturalness/spontaneity.
![Guy meditating Image](/images/daoism_meditation.png)
Things are best when allowed to unfold according to their own nature instead of being shaped by artificial conventions, laws, or social performance.
:::

::: article 
## Pu (樸) — The "uncarved block"
![Guy meditating Image](/images/daoism_authentic.png)
Simplicity, authenticity, and a return to one's original, unconditioned state. Letting go of excess desire, cleverness, and accumulated artifice.
:::
